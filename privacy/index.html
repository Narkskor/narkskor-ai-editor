Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NARKSKOR AI EDITOR ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when a user uses the desktop application and connects supported third-party services.

1. Information the App may process

  • Local video and audio files selected by the user.
  • Transcripts, subtitles, project metadata, render settings, and publishing metadata generated from user content.
  • OAuth authorization data and access tokens for connected third-party services such as YouTube or TikTok.
  • Basic account information returned by connected services when required for authentication or publishing.

2. How information is used

  • Analyze, edit, render, and organize user-selected video content.
  • Create previews, subtitles, titles, and other editing outputs.
  • Authenticate with services selected by the user.
  • Upload or publish content only when the user initiates or confirms that action.
  • Display publishing history and statistics inside the App.

3. Local storage

Project files, settings, cached previews, transcripts, and publishing history may be stored locally on the user's device. Authentication tokens may also be stored locally so the user does not need to sign in every time.

4. Third-party services

If the user connects a third-party platform, the App may communicate with that platform using its official APIs. The platform's own terms and privacy policies also apply. The App does not sell user data.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Data is shared with a connected service only when required to perform an action requested by the user, such as authentication, uploading, publishing, or retrieving video statistics.

6. Data retention and removal

Local project data and tokens remain on the user's device until deleted by the user or removed by the App's cleanup features. Users may also revoke third-party access from the relevant platform's account settings.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to limit access to locally stored authentication credentials and project data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's privacy

NARKSKOR AI EDITOR is not directed to children under the minimum age required by the connected platform or applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or integrations change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions about NARKSKOR AI EDITOR, contact the developer through the official NARKSKOR channels associated with the application.
